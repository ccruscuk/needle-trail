Privacy Policy for Needle Trail

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Needle Trail ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone made by BeeGame ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Summary

  • The game sends gameplay data tied to a random install code, not to your name, to an analytics service so we can find bugs and balance the levels. In some regions the game asks for your permission first (see "Your choices").
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. There is no account.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • A random install code that the game creates on your iPhone the first time you open it. It is not linked to your name. The first six characters are shown in the game's info panel.
  • Gameplay events: for example when you start, finish or leave a level, the level number, how long it took, how many pieces of thread you undid, how many times you reset a level, and whether you used the hint.
  • Basic technical data: app version and build, device type and iOS version, your IP address and the approximate country derived from it, and a device identifier that is the same for all apps from the same developer on your device (the identifier for vendors).

We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and the game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Why we collect it

To understand how many people play, where levels are too hard or confusing, and to fix problems. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with data from other companies.

Who processes it

The data is processed for us by Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics), acting as our service provider ("processor") under its data processing terms. Unity's servers are mainly located in the European Union, with a replicated copy in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside your country. We receive this data from Unity only in the form of reports and statistics. We do not share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Unity keeps raw event data for 13 months and then deletes it automatically. Some summary statistics linked to your install code may be kept until they are deleted on request.

Your choices and deleting your data

Where permission is required: if the region setting of your device is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Turkey (or the game cannot tell), the game asks on first launch whether you agree to gameplay analytics. Until you choose, no analytics data is sent from your device. If you choose "No thanks", you can play the whole game and no analytics data is sent.

Elsewhere: analytics is on by default.

Changing your mind: in every region you can turn analytics on or off at any time in the game's info panel ("Analytics: On / Off"). When you turn it off, the game stops sending data and sends a request to delete the data already linked to your install code.

Other requests: you can also ask us to delete the data linked to your install code. Send an email to info@beemap.io and include the code shown in the game's info panel. Deleting the game from your iPhone stops further data collection; it does not by itself delete data that was already sent, so please email us if you want it removed. You can also ask us what data we hold about your code.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and is not in the Kids category. It contains no ads, chat or purchases. We do not ask for or knowingly collect personal information such as names or email addresses from anyone. The data described above is collected in the same way from every player whose analytics is on. If you are a parent or guardian and want data linked to a code removed, email us.

Internet connection

The game can be played without an internet connection. When a connection is available and analytics is on, the data described above is sent to Unity. The only link the game opens is this privacy policy, which opens in your web browser (Safari).

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
